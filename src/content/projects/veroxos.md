## Overview

Veroxos is StableLogic's enterprise platform for telecom expense management, mobility management and device asset lifecycle — including secure disposal. Enterprise IT, procurement and telecom teams use it to cut costs on carriers and devices.

## My contribution

- Worked on the platform's **AI capabilities**, which help teams query and act on telecom and device data in plain language.
- Built **new modules** on the existing product.

## Context

Veroxos is a mature product with many companies already using it, so the work had to fit an established architecture, compliance requirements and existing customers' workflows.

## Outcome

The features I worked on shipped into a live product that companies use to save on their telecom and device costs.
