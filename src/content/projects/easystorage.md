## Overview

EasyStorage, part of the easyGroup family, offers low-cost self storage across the UK in three ways: collect-and-store, drop-off and drive-up. The booking platform is where customers estimate how much space they need, get a quote and book.

## The problem

Three different service models, regional store coverage, promotional pricing and a space calculator all had to come together in one checkout that ordinary customers could finish without calling anyone. Every extra step in the funnel cost orders.

## My contribution

- Built the **booking platform from scratch** and took it **to production**.
- Implemented the **quote and booking flow** across the different storage services.
- Integrated **pricing, promotions and payments** into a single checkout.
- Worked with the business to keep the focus on **conversion and order volume**.

## Technical approach

Pricing and availability rules live in the backend instead of the page templates, so offers and service options can change without reworking the user journey. The booking flow is kept short and works well on mobile, where most consumer traffic arrives.

## Outcome

The platform **increased orders** and was delivered as a **profitable** channel for the business.
