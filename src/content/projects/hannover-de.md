## Overview

hannover.de is the official portal of the state capital Hannover and the Hannover Region. Residents, visitors and businesses use it for citizen services, events, tourism, maps and public information, in around 20 languages.

## My contribution

- Developed **frontend and backend modules** for the portal.
- Worked on **eZ Platform** (built on Symfony) against a **large content database**.
- Followed the team's **TDD and BDD** practices with PHPUnit and Behat.

Some of the work was internal to the municipal project and isn't described here.

## Outcome

The modules shipped as part of a long-running public platform that serves a whole city region.
